    GAME RULES

    THE THREE MAN

    The Three Man is chosen at the start by the spin wheel.

    ▸Anyone (not the Three Man) rolls a 3 on any die or a sum of 3 — the Three Man takes a sip. The roller goes again.
    ▸Roller gets doubles including a 3 (e.g. 3+3) — the Three Man takes TWO sips.
    ▸The Three Man rolls a 3 — they choose who gets the title next (or keep it).
    ▸The Three Man rolls double 3s — they MUST pass the title first, then distribute the dice.

    DOUBLES

    Roll the same number twice? Pick one:

    1Pass 1 die to 2 people — each rolls and drinks their result.
    2Pass both dice to 1 person — they roll and drink the sum.

    THE CYCLE — If the recipients both roll the same number, they escape! Dice return to you. If they match again, the multiplier grows:

    ▸1st match — 3×
    ▸2nd match — 4×
    ▸3rd match — 5× (and up)

    When they finally roll different numbers, each drinks their roll × the current multiplier.

    SPECIAL ROLLS

    7Player to your RIGHT drinks.
    9EVERYONE drinks.
    11Player to your LEFT drinks.

    ANYTHING ELSE

    Nothing happens. Turn passes to the next player.

    DOUBLE TROUBLE
    When doubles are rolled, everyone drinks instead of just the chosen players.
    CHAIN REACTION
    Rolling 7 or 11 hits BOTH neighbors — left AND right drink.
    HOT POTATO
    Match the previous roller's total and they drink again!
    IMMUNITY ROUND
    Three Man gets 1 free block per game — they can skip one drink.
    BOUNTY
    First player to make the Three Man drink 5 times steals the title.
    REVERSE ROUND
    7 and 11 flip — 7 hits left, 11 hits right.
    SOCIAL 9
    Rolling a 9 makes everyone drink.
    SOCIAL OVERLOAD
    Both 6 and 9 trigger a social — everyone drinks.
