    GAME RULES

    THREE MAN TITLE

    The Three Man is decided at the start of the game by the spinner wheel.

    If anyone (who is NOT the Three Man) rolls a 3 on any die, the Three Man takes a sip. The roller then rolls again.

    If the roller gets doubles that include a 3 (e.g. 3+3), the Three Man takes TWO sips.

    The Three Man title only switches when the Three Man themselves rolls a 3. They choose who gets the title next.

    If the Three Man rolls double 3s, they FIRST pass the title, THEN choose how to distribute the dice (Option 1 or 2).

    DOUBLES

    Roll the same number twice? You have two options:

    Option 1: Pass 1 die to 2 people. Each rolls and drinks for their result.

    Option 2: Pass both dice to 1 person. They roll and drink for the sum.

    THE CYCLE: If the 2 people who received dice both roll the SAME number, they escape drinking! Dice go back to you.

    You roll again, and if they match again, the multiplier increases:
    • 1st match: 3x multiplier
    • 2nd match: 4x multiplier
    • 3rd match: 5x multiplier (and so on...)

    Each match continues the cycle. When they finally roll different numbers, they drink their individual roll × current multiplier!

    LUCKY 7

    Rolled a 7? The player to your RIGHT drinks.

    ELEVEN

    Rolled an 11? The player to your LEFT drinks.

    SOCIAL 9

    Rolled a 9? EVERYONE drinks!

    ANYTHING ELSE

    Nothing special happens. Turn passes to the next player.
